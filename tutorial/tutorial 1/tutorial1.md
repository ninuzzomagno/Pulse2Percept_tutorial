## How much do you see?
With your eyes, you have a large visual field(VF) which is not equal for vertical and horizontal direction. If you consider only your right eye(RE), your VF covers almost 160° in horizontal and 120° in vertical direction. Using both your eyes you can reach almost 180° along horizontal direction. Nevertheless, we can see the world with the maximum resolution only near a little spot in the center of our VF, called FOVEA. When we move away from the fovea, our visual acuity drop almost instant. So you have a visual acuity of almost 10/10 in your fovea(if you don't have any visual issue ), but, at almost 60° your acuity drop to 1/60!!
